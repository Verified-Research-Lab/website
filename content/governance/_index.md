policies:
  - title: "Sponsorship and conflicts"
    summary: "Funding is disclosed and cannot determine the test or conclusion."
    rules:
      - "Sponsors may define the decision or claim that needs examination, but they may not direct methods, suppress material results, or rewrite the conclusion."
      - "Reports disclose funding, relevant relationships, competence limits, and whether publication was part of the agreed scope."
      - "A conflict or prior advisory role is disclosed before work begins and may require a different evaluator or refusal of the engagement."
    client: "You know before engagement which decisions remain yours, which judgments belong to VRL, and what sponsorship information will appear in any public record."
  - title: "Review and independent sign-off"
    summary: "Subjects can correct facts; they cannot veto the finding."
    rules:
      - "The subject receives a defined factual-review window before a public report is released."
      - "Factual corrections are incorporated when supported. A subject response and evaluator reply may be published with the report."
      - "High-stakes public findings receive a second review for traceability, scope, and conclusion language."
    client: "The process catches factual errors without turning review into approval. Publication timing and response handling are agreed before testing starts."
  - title: "Evidence, correction, and withdrawal"
    summary: "The record shows what changed without erasing what came before."
    rules:
      - "Methods and raw evidence are preserved when legal, contractual, confidentiality, and security obligations permit."
      - "Each public report identifies its version, correction history, and current revalidation status."
      - "Amendments, supersession, and withdrawal remain visible with the reason and effective date."
    client: "Readers can tell which version is current, what evidence remains available, and whether later information changed the conclusion."
